Paris

Paris is the capital of France, known for art, fashion and food.

Eiffel Tower

Built in 1889, it is the most iconic landmark of Paris.
Visitors can climb or take a lift to enjoy the city view.

Louvre Museum

The world's largest art museum.
It is home to the Mona Lisa.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

A masterpiece of Gothic architecture.
It stands on an island in the Seine river.
